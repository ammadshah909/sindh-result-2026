Result kaise check karein?

Home page par "Check Your Result" button dabayein. Wahan apna 6-digit roll number daal kar result dekh sakte hain.

Roll number nahi mil raha?

Agar aapka roll number result data mein nahi milta, toh baraye meharbani apne college se rabta karein ya humein email karein.

Total marks kitne hain?

Intermediate Science (XI) ke total marks 550 hain (5 papers × 110). Pass hone ke liye kam az kam 33% marks zaroori hain.

Koi fees hai?

Nahi, bilkul free hai. Koi fees nahi. Aap jitni baar chahein apna result check kar sakte hain.

Result kab tak available?

Result 2026 ke poore saal available rahega. Aap jab chahein apna result dekh sakte hain.

Data authentic hai?

Ji haan, 100% authentic. Yeh result data seedha Sindh Board se liya gaya hai. Har roll number verified hai.
