Sindh Board of Intermediate Education

Sindh Board of Intermediate Education (SBIE) Sindh province ka aik ahem idara hai jo intermediate (Class XI aur XII) ki taleem aur imtihanat ka zimmedar hai. Yeh board 1974 mein qaim kiya gaya tha aur tab se lakhon students ko behtar taleem faraham kar raha hai.

Har saal ki tarah, 2026 ke Annual Examination mein bhi Science General (XI) group ke tamam students ke results is portal ke zariye online available karaye ja rahe hain. Hamara maqsad hai ke har student apna result foran, asaani se aur bharose ke saath check kar sake.

"Taleem woh roshni hai jo har ghar tak pohanchni chahiye — bilkul free, bilkul asaan."

Mission, Vision & Values

Woh usool jo humein aage barhate hain

Hamara Mission

Har student ko apna result foran, asaani se aur bharose ke saath check karne ki sahulat faraham karna. Taleem ko sab ke liye accessible banana — bina kisi rukawat ke.

Hamara Vision

Sindh Board ke tamam results ko digital platform par le kar aana, taake students ko kisi bhi mushkil ka samna na karna pade. Har ghar tak taleem ki roshni pohanchana.

Hamari Values

Shafafiyat, bharosa, aur asaani. Har roll number authentic hai, har result official data par mabni hai — koi milawat nahi, koi jhoot nahi.
